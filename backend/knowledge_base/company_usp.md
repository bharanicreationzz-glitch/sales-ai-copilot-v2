# Company & Trust Guidance

knowledge_type: company_usp

Use factual company positioning where relevant:
- Professional project handling
- Transparent WhatsApp updates
- Hassle-free execution
- Fast installation
- Dedicated civil engineer supervision

Sales rule:
Use company strengths to support trust. Do not replace requirement discovery with generic company claims.

Never invent:
- Certifications
- Warranty terms
- Number of projects
- Number of customers
- Guarantees
- Competitor weaknesses
