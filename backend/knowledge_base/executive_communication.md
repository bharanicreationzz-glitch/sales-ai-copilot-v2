# Executive Communication Coaching

knowledge_type: communication

## Strong speech

Should be:
- Clear
- Calm
- Direct
- Professional
- Concise
- Helpful

## Weak patterns

Watch for:
- Excessive "maybe"
- Excessive "I think"
- "Probably"
- "Basically"
- "I guess"
- Long explanations
- Repeating the same point
- Uncertainty about known information
- Filler-heavy speech

## Better alternatives

Weak:
"I think maybe this product should be okay."

Better:
"Based on what you've told me, this option appears suitable. Let me confirm the exact specification before I finalize it."

Weak:
"I don't know, I'll check."

Better:
"Let me verify that and get back to you with the confirmed information."

Weak:
"Maybe we can give some discount."

Better:
"Let me understand the quantity and project requirement first. Then I'll check the applicable pricing."

## Confidence rule

Confidence is not false certainty.

"I'll verify that and confirm" can be high-quality professional communication.

## Response structure

ANSWER + REASON + NEXT STEP
