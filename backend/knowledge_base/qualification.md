# Customer Qualification

knowledge_type: qualification

## Required discovery

- Location
- Property/site type
- Boundary length
- Required height
- Purpose
- Quantity
- Product preference
- Installation requirement
- Timeline
- Decision-maker

## Customer category

Dealer:
- Dealership
- Distributor
- Reseller
- Stockist
- Retailer
- Hardware shop
- Resale requirement

B2B:
- Builder
- Contractor
- Developer
- Construction company
- Factory
- Warehouse
- School
- Institution
- Company
- Commercial project
- Buying for client projects

B2C:
- Individual personal property
- Individual farmer's own land
- House
- Plot
- Farmhouse
- Garden
- Personal agricultural land

If evidence is insufficient:
NA

## Lead potential

Hot:
- Wants to proceed
- Wants to place order
- Requests final quotation
- Immediate timeline
- Confirms requirement
- Wants installation arranged

Warm:
- Genuine requirement
- Exploring
- Comparing
- Asking pricing
- Product pending
- Needs follow-up

Cold:
- Casual enquiry
- Very little interest
- No genuine requirement

Do not classify Hot from salesperson behavior alone.
