# Sales Guidance

knowledge_type: sales_guidance

## Core sales flow

UNDERSTAND -> QUALIFY -> RECOMMEND -> EXPLAIN VALUE -> HANDLE CONCERNS -> REDUCE DECISION FRICTION -> CONFIRM NEXT STEP

## Discovery

Confirm where possible:
- Location
- Property/site type
- Boundary length
- Required height
- Fencing purpose
- Product preference
- Quantity
- Installation requirement
- Timeline
- Decision-maker
- Budget sensitivity
- Main concern

Core questions:
- "Which location is the site in?"
- "What type of property is this?"
- "Approximately how many feet is the boundary?"
- "What height are you looking for?"
- "What is the main purpose — security, privacy, appearance, or land protection?"
- "Do you need installation support as well?"
- "When are you planning to start?"

## Discovery before pitching

Do not begin with a product dump.

Weak:
"PolyHex is rust-free and very strong..."

Better:
"May I know whether this is for agricultural land, a house, or a commercial property?"

Then connect product benefits to the actual need.

## Value-based selling

FEATURE -> BENEFIT -> CUSTOMER USE CASE

Examples:
- Rust-free -> less concern about rust-related maintenance.
- Lightweight -> easier handling and installation.
- Modern design -> better appearance for a visible property boundary.
- Artificial greenery -> green appearance without watering/trimming.
- High-strength knot fence -> suitable where strong containment is important.

## Recommendation

Use customer purpose as the starting point:
- Agricultural + lightweight + rust-free -> consider PolyHex Mesh.
- Agricultural + high strength + livestock/wildlife -> consider Checks Mesh.
- Fencing support poles -> consider Trinity Poles.
- Modern design + boundary -> consider Lato Fence.
- Privacy + green appearance -> consider Privezy Grass Wall.
- Premium decorative appearance -> consider Antiqo Fence.

These are guidance signals, not automatic decisions.

## Executive communication

Strong executive speech is:
- Clear
- Calm
- Direct
- Professional
- Helpful
- Certain about known facts
- Honest about unknown information

Avoid excessive:
- Maybe
- I think
- Probably
- Basically
- I guess

Better:
"Based on what you've told me, this option appears suitable. Let me confirm the exact specification before I finalize it."

When information is unknown:
"Let me verify that and get back to you with the confirmed information."

Confidence does not mean pretending to know everything.

## Closing

Never end with only:
"Okay sir."

Close the next small step:
- Confirm boundary length.
- Confirm height.
- Send site photos.
- Share quotation.
- Arrange site visit.
- Confirm product preference.
- Agree on follow-up date.

Use:
WHAT + WHEN + NEXT ACTION.

## Avoid

- Pitching before discovery.
- Quoting final price without sufficient dimensions/location.
- Treating salesperson statements as customer decisions.
- Automatically converting generic product names.
- Immediate discounting after price objections.
- Criticizing competitors.
- Fake urgency.
- Repeated generic follow-ups.
- Arguing.
- Unsupported claims.
- Ending without a clear next step.
